❓ How to Play

1

Select a Puzzle

Choose from 50 levels across 10 difficulty tiers. Start with easy 12-piece puzzles and work your way up to 256-piece legends!

2

Drag & Drop Pieces

Click or touch a piece to pick it up. Drag it to the puzzle board. On mobile, pieces are sized for easy touch interaction.

3

Rotate When Needed

Harder levels have rotated pieces. Use the ↻ Rotate button, right-click, or double-tap to rotate a piece 90°.

4

Magnetic Snap

When a piece is close to its correct position, it will snap into place with a satisfying animation. Connected pieces form groups!

5

Use Hints Wisely

Hints are limited! They can highlight the next piece, show ghost placement, or auto-place a piece. Save them for tough spots.

6

Earn Stars & Unlock

Complete puzzles quickly and without hints to earn 3 stars. Unlock new levels, images, and themes as you progress!
